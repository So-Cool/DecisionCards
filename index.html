Decision Cards

Map a decision problem across frame, strategy and choice, record the role of AI and save the card as a PDF. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Preview

“Save as PDF” opens your browser’s print dialog. Choose “Save as PDF” as the destination and turn off headers and footers for a clean page.

Based on Michaela Benk and Kacper Sokol, Decision Cards: An Analytical Tool for Mapping Decision Problems and Designing AI Support Systems.

The example cards are illustrations drawn from published descriptions of prior studies, not authoritative specifications of those systems. Your draft is kept in this browser only; share links contain the whole card in the URL.
